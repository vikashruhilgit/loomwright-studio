# 23d — The charter card's tracker panel hangs on its first open

## Status: proposed

**Observed live (2026-11-16, setup step 6, app built from `15a865b1`, freshly launched).** On approval 45's card in Chat, Choose access showed "Reading ClickUp's tools…" for over a minute and never loaded. Closing and reopening the panel loaded it in under 5 s. The same kernel call (`GET /connectors`) answered from the CLI in 0.27 s at that moment, and the ClickUp connection was healthy (token to 2036). The panel (`useTrackerConnection`, `app/src/screens/chat/CharterCard.tsx:101`) has no timeout and no retry, so a request that never settles leaves the owner stuck. Not reproduced a second time; the first-open condition is unknown.

**Ask.** Reproduce first (memory: a defect claim needs a reproduction before it becomes a fix requirement); then the panel times out to its existing "Couldn't read the ClickUp connection" state with a retry.

**Code read, 2026-11-17 (main at `7184d57d`), not a reproduction.** The request the panel waits on is already bounded: `GET /connectors` goes renderer → `ipcMain.handle(CHANNELS.request)` (`app/electron/main.ts:74`) → `createKernelConnection.request`, whose keychain token read times out at 10 s (`KEYCHAIN_READ_TIMEOUT_MS`) and whose fetch carries `AbortSignal.timeout(REQUEST_TIMEOUT_MS)` = 10 s (`app/electron/kernel-connection.ts:265`), answering `unreachable: timeout`, which the panel shows as its "Couldn't read" state. So a slow kernel call cannot hold "Reading ClickUp's tools…" for over a minute; the observed hang is something else (the IPC promise itself, or the effect being re-run and its answer dropped by `live = false` before it lands). The bridge object is stable (`window.api`), which makes the second less likely. A renderer-side timeout would hide whichever it is, so none was added. **Next:** reproduce on a fresh launch of a packaged build with a held ClickUp ticket-duty charter, with the renderer's devtools open, and record whether the `request` IPC call was sent and whether it settled.
