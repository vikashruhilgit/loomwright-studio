# Research: naming

Decided (D14): **Loomwright Studio** (the app) + **Wright** (the lead agent). Fallback: **Clotho**. The clash checks below are web searches from 2026-09-27/28. They are **not** a trademark clearance (see `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`).

## Why Wright

A *wright* is the master craftsman who owns the workshop, decides what gets made and directs the workers. That is the role of the lead agent. Loomwright is the craft, Loomwright Studio is the workshop, Wright is the master. Because it lives inside the owner's brand, it doesn't need to be unique on the web.

**Known drawbacks:**
- **Wright vs Loomwright could be confused.** Keep the roles strict in UI and docs: *Loomwright* = the toolkit, *Wright* = the one who uses it.
- **It isn't distinctive as a standalone brand.** It would need a new name if the agent were ever spun off as its own product.
